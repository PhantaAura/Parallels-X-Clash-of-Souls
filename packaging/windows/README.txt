Move / menu: WASD or Arrow Keys

Dash: Left Shift or Right Shift
Light: J
Heavy: K
Launcher: I
Grab: U
Block: L
Counter: Q
Breaker: R
Charge: C
Interact: E
Abilities: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

Mouse: Left = Light, Right = Block
