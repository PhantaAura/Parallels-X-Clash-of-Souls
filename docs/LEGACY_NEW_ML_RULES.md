# Legacy + Current Canon + Mario & Luigi Inspiration

This document is a guardrail for 3.0R, not a redesign pitch.

## Take from the Legacy

Preserve the player's remembered game identity:

- Chapter 1 Training Region / Tournament Road geography and route logic.
- authored hubs rather than empty open zones.
- Light 1→2→3, Heavy, Launcher, Air routes, Grab, Block, Perfect Block, Counter, Breaker, Charge, true Dash, Pursuit and Flow Cancel.
- ability hotbar and character-specific techniques.
- optional interactions and fight-or-leave encounters.
- old dialogue/personality whenever it still matches current canon.
- the Tournament Card, Old Man's Potion quest and Ryuzankaro as protected features.

Do not inherit browser/mobile patch layering, chapter-specific engines, giant HUD clutter or obsolete story contradictions.

## Take from current story/canon

Current story documents overrule Legacy chronology when the old game conflicts with them. Chapter 1 starts at Sage's field, does not use a recovery arc, does not invent Shots of Agony, and ends at the tournament outskirts. Tournament registration/Card belongs to Chapter 2.

## Take inspiration from Mario & Luigi

Use the *world-design philosophy*, not Nintendo/AlphaDream code or assets:

- Superstar Saga-style compact interconnected areas, loops, shortcuts, NPC density and field interactions.
- Dream Team-style presentation polish, landmarks, animated scenery and larger setpieces.
- on 3DS, top screen = world/combat/cutscenes; bottom screen = map/RPG/menu/hotbar/Tournament Card information.

The actual geography, quests, combat and character voice remain Parallels X.
