# Parallels X 3.0R — Mac 0.4C: Grand Adventure I

## Why this patch exists

The Legacy build still wins on fun because its Chapter 1 already behaves like a trip: training grounds, road, broken river, three routes, field problems, relay, roadside life, Lens roadblock and tournament arrival. 0.4C begins rebuilding that playable rhythm inside the clean shared engine instead of merely preserving the coordinates.

## Legacy behavior deliberately preserved

- the river is a real blocker and never becomes ordinary floor;
- Object Swap is the crossing solution;
- Main / Forest / Cliff are physical branches that reconnect;
- Main Road uses Fire Blast to solve the fallen-log problem;
- Object Swap gets a precision relay after the initial field use;
- Lens offers a shortcut while a physical detour remains possible;
- Chapter 1 still ends at Tournament Outskirts rather than stealing Chapter 2 registration/card content.

## Remake improvement in this patch

Legacy presented the three roads through a `CHOOSE A ROUTE` menu. 3.0R makes the geography itself the choice: walk the center, lower, or upper branch and the shared runtime records that route.
